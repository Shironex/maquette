# v0.2.0: animations, parts, refs by view and a style board

The first update since the public release. It adds a library of animation clips you can bake onto
any rigged model, splitting a model into named parts, refs tagged by view, and a style board every
concept job follows. It also comes with an accessibility and performance pass and every dependency
brought up to date. It is still early: back up your workspace before you update.

## Highlights

- **An Animations library** next to Models: import Mixamo FBX files or whole pack zips once, then
  bake clips onto any rigged model as its next version, with a review of every clip before it
  lands.
- **Parts:** split a model that came back as one mesh into named parts, in Blender for free or with
  Tripo or Meshy, then rename, merge and hide parts and compare before and after.
- **Assemble a prop** from part models, placed by numbers or by moving them in the viewer.
- **Refs by view:** tag each ref as front, side, back or top, draw the missing views, and get a
  warning when a ref is too small, cropped, busy or does not match the front.
- **A style board per project** that every Lab job carries.
- **Downloads that match more often:** a close match asks first, and any download can be attached
  to the asset it belongs to.

## What's new

### Animations library

The rail has a Libraries group with Models and Animations. Import reads Mixamo FBX files or pack
zips headless in Blender and keeps only the skeleton and keys, about 80 KB a clip; files with no
keys are left out with the reason. Clips are tagged from their names and grouped by pack, with a
preview, root motion and loop marks. Mixamo clips stay on your computer and leave it only baked into
a model.

### Use on model

Choose clips, check the bone map, set each clip's settings, then review a bake on a copy before
anything is saved. The review flags clips to look at (feet sliding, hips leaving, limbs turned from
the rest pose). **Pin the feet and bake again** holds each planted foot in place with leg IK and
shows the slide before and after. **Open in Blender** opens the draft in a Blender window when you
want to look closer. The bake becomes the model's next version.

### Parts

A model that came back as one mesh gets **Split into parts**: by loose pieces or by material in
Blender, or with Tripo's segmentation or Meshy's Auto Split as a paid send with its own approval.
Meshy's split drops the texture, so the previous version's texture bakes back on its own. The
Inspector's Parts tab colours each part and lets you rename, merge and hide them, and shows before
and after with draw calls, triangles and transfer. Game projects get an explode slider.

### Assemble a prop

Build one prop from several part models: node names, order, a pivot each (a door on its hinge) and
placement where the models put them, by numbers, or by dragging a move or turn gizmo in the viewer
with snapping.

### Refs by view

Each ref can be tagged with its view. Make the other views draws the missing ones, and Clean
background cuts a busy one. Before a send, Maquette warns when a ref is too small, cropped, has a
busy background, is the only view, or when a side or back looks different from the front in colour
or proportions.

### Style board

`style.json` per project holds a line about the look, a palette and up to four images. Every Lab job
carries it, so a project's concepts stay in one style.

### Downloads

A download whose name is close to an asset's, but not the same, waits for you to confirm it. A
download that matches nothing can be attached to any asset.

### Agents

New MCP tools for all of the above, from 65 to 85: style board, ref views, parts, compare, splits,
assembly, animation import and use, the retarget review, bake back and attaching downloads.

## Accessibility

- Dialogs and sheets keep keyboard focus inside and give it back to what opened them; menus do the
  same.
- Muted text and the clay and sage chips are a little lighter, so they read at 4.5:1 or better.
- Tab lists move with the arrow keys and take one Tab press.
- 3D views are labelled for screen readers, camera moves respect reduced motion, and the Inbox
  preview's turntable can be stopped.
- The pipeline's step and failures are announced.

## Performance

- Long lists render only what is in view: the Board, the Board list, both libraries, the Inbox,
  the run log and the credits ledger. With 500 cards, moving through the Board list with the
  keyboard went from about 300 ms to 15 ms.
- three.js and every view but the Board load when first opened: the first load is about 265 kB
  gzip, down from about 455 kB.
- The model previews free a model and its textures when they show another one.

## Bug fixes

- The library grids showed a few cards and blank space until the view was switched.
- A Windows test of the preview server could race its listener.

## Under the hood

- Tauri 2.12, zip 9, sha2 0.11, vitest 5, React 19.3 and the rest of the dependencies, with the
  open advisories fixed.
- CI checks every commit message in a pull request, and `pnpm a11y` runs axe over the app's views.

## Install

Download the `.dmg` (macOS, Apple silicon) or the `-setup.exe` (Windows x64) below, or update from
the app. The builds are not signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so a first install shows Gatekeeper's
"Open Anyway" or SmartScreen's "Run anyway" once. Install guide:
https://shironex.github.io/maquette/docs/start/install/
